How it works For shops Drive with KiriGo

Mauri. Your order, brought down the road to you.

Shop with KiriPay or any KiriPOS shop and choose delivery. Pick your driver by price and rating, watch them come to you, and KiriPay holds your money until the order is in your hands.

Delivery appears at checkout in the KiriPay app.

How a delivery works

  1. Choose a driver

    At checkout you see the drivers near the shop: their price, rating, vehicle and plate. You pick.

  2. Pay once, held safely

    You pay for the goods and the delivery together. KiriPay holds it; nobody is paid yet.

  3. Watch it come

    Follow your driver on the map from the shop to your door.

  4. Check, then confirm

    Show your code, check the order, tap “Received, all correct”. Only then is the shop paid.

Wrong item? Send it back. The shop pays for the return and you get your money back, delivery charge included.

100%of every delivery charge is yours

Drive with KiriGo

Motorbike, car, van or truck. Set your own prices, choose the jobs you take, and get paid into your KiriPay wallet the moment you hand an order over.

You pay a small monthly fee, and the first 3 months are free.

Sign up to drive

You'll need a KiriPay account with your ID checked, two-step sign-in, and a driver's licence for a motor vehicle.

For KiriPOS shops

Turn on delivery in KiriPOS settings and pin where drivers collect. Orders arrive on your Orders page already paid and held, and you're paid when the customer confirms.
